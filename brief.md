- Task: write ONE sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a
  friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea
  on make.fun. It is the whole entry: no code, no explanation.
- Judging: a house judge compares sentences two at a time on
  persuasiveness, clarity and truth in one read, in private blind rounds.
  Criteria in order: makes the case to that friend in one read;
  persuasion (concrete reason to say yes now, easy and low risk);
  clarity (plain words, no jargon); truth (no overpromising); warmth
  and craft (sounds like a person talking to a friend, fresh turn of
  phrase, not a template or slogan).
- Deliverable: `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the pitch to
  `/output/entry.txt`; a sealed scorer checks the file is UTF-8 text of
  1–240 characters, not only whitespace. Only a valid entry goes before
  the judge.
- Entry must be original — written from scratch in this session, not
  copied from examples, other entries, or any earlier attempt.

  34233208); LICENSE MIT; README carries the three make.fun badges.
